Brand Test Tool (BTT)
Validate payment devices before connecting to payment networks
Perform the brand testing easily and successfully
Payment schemes mandate proper testing before payment devices may connect to their network infrastructure. This is to ensure interoperability and an EMV®-compliant, payment association certified terminal performing trouble-free transactions within your payment infrastructure.
Brand Test Tool (BTT) assists in performing the brand testing processes and provides all mandatory tests implemented for Point of Sale terminals, Mobile POS devices, Automated Teller Machines, Vending machines and other payment devices.
